# Code of Conduct

Continuity welcomes contributors regardless of background, identity, experience,
or employer. Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Debate technical decisions
on their merits. Respect privacy and accept reasonable requests to stop.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, impersonation,
and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban participants in
proportion to the severity and recurrence of behavior. Reports will be reviewed
with discretion; identities and private information will not be published without
consent except where legally required.

To report conduct privately, contact the repository owner through the contact
channels on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/cubiix3). If no private contact
channel is available, use the repository's private advisory form with the title
"Private conduct report"; do not put personal details in a public issue.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other
spaces officially maintained by the project.
